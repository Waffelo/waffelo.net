Waffelo is a curious furred dragon with subtle purple undertones and accent color. Two big horns between his ears purple stripped ears, containing fluffy purple hair in between. He has striking yellow eyes. Long snout with sharp fangs, which one of them is replaced with golden one after an injury.

In the back he has a pair of strong wings that help him transport himself occasionally. He usualy wears tail-bags to carry different tools and gear. Long tail in his waist with purple zig-zag pattern and big fluff ball on the end.
