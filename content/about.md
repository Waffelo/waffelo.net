## My systems
<hr class="hr-wide">

### Enviroment
My operating system of choice is mainly __Gentoo Linux__, I run it everywhere except at work, I have Debian there instead. I like the customazibility of it and that it is purple :>. I do not use any floating window desktop enviroment, I prefrer tiling window managers. I currently am using the __Sway__ window manager which is for the Wayland video server. I might try something else again like Hyprland or going back to DWM.

As of software, I use __Alacritty__ as my primary terminal emulator and I try to use mostly commandline interface programs such as __Neovim__, ranger, sxiv, etc.. My default browser is Librewolf, which is just stripped down Firefox modified to be more private, but it is kinda wonky sometimes, so I use the Brave browser for some tasks like web flashing of Meshtastic nodes.

<br>

### __Main computer__
{{< optimized-img src="images/fastfetch.png" alt="Desktop fastfetch" width="350" class="float-right">}}

I have a custom build computer with Asus mATX motherboard. I went full AMD with this build, which to me is the only true chad option. My CPU is __Ryzen 7 5700X__ which is pretty awesome for me. I have __32GB of DDR4 DIMMs__, which is enough for my Gentoo Linux compilation activities, but I might upgrade to 64GB or 128GB. My GPU is __Radeon 6650XT__ with 8GB of VRAM. It is pretty much enough for me, I am not a huge gamer, I play videogames ocassionaly, and even then it is mostly just older titles. For cooling I use a chonker Noctua tower cooler and I have 3 intake fans in the front, for exhaust I have two on the top of the case and one in the back. Seems to work well for me. I have quite a lot of storage, I have 2TB disk for personal files and another 2TB for media like games, movies and pictures. My Gentoo system is on one of the 500GB NVME drives. I think my system would be fine even on 250GB one. One NVME is unused because I don't know what to put on it lol.

## My 3D printer


{{< optimized-img src="images/voron.jpg" alt="Voron v2.4" width="320" class="float-right">}}
For 3D printing I have decided to get a Voron V2.4 r2 with 350x350mm build plate. I got it from a Fysetc kit and I must say, what an adventure to build this thing. I took days off work to build it and it took me like 3 days of almost nonstop assembly. I must say that it has taught me a lot more about how good 3D printers are built and how they work. I also love that the 3D printer is entirely open-source, I would __never__ get a stupid BambuLab printer.
